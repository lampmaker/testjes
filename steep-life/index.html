a still life you can drink

Oil on canvas, hot water, patience.
Visitors are kindly requested to touch the painting.

tap · hold to pour · draw circles to stir
sound on, if the room allows

How to make tea in a painting

  • Hold the stoneware jug to fill the kettle.
  • Tap the kettle to switch it on. Hold it to pour.
  • Tap a tin to choose a tea. Each label says what it likes.
  • Tap the tag to dunk the bag; hold it to lift the bag out.
  • Hold the creamer for milk. Tap sugar, lemon, stroopwafel.
  • Draw circles in the cup to stir.
  • Tap the cup to sip. It leaves the painting briefly.

There is no wrong way. The critic will have opinions regardless.
If you stop touching it, the paint dries.
